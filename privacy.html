Spoolbook privacy policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

What Spoolbook stores

Your printers, spools, quotes, prices and business details are saved only on your own device, in the app's local storage. Spoolbook has no accounts and no server that receives your data.

Sliced files

When you open a .gcode or .3mf file, Spoolbook reads it on your PC to find filament use and print time. The file is not uploaded anywhere and is not kept.

What Spoolbook sends

Spoolbook does not collect analytics, use advertising identifiers or share data with third parties. If you buy Spoolbook Pro, the purchase is handled by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement; Spoolbook only asks the Store whether your Microsoft account owns Pro.

Removing your data

Delete quotes and spools inside the app, or uninstall Spoolbook to remove everything it stored on your device.
